STRUCTURE (immutable -- identical in every floor of this building):
- ONE single storey of a cutaway building, drawn in isometric projection.
- The cut face is PARALLEL to the picture plane: back walls are flat and face
  the viewer square on, partition walls are vertical, and the row of rooms runs
  straight across the frame as a horizontal band. Do NOT rotate the building,
  do NOT converge the side walls, do NOT draw a dollhouse corner view.
- Depth is shallow: only floors, ceilings, ledges and props show isometric
  recession, going back and up to the right at 30 degrees, by a small amount.
- The building fills the frame completely, edge to edge and top to bottom. The
  storey BLEEDS OFF all four edges of the image. Do not draw the whole building,
  do not shrink it to fit, do not leave a margin, border, or empty band anywhere.
- Vertically the storey EXACTLY fills the image: the top edge of the image cuts
  straight through the ceiling slab, and the bottom edge cuts straight through
  this storey's own floor slab. There is NO black above the ceiling and NO black
  below the floor -- the ceiling and floor run off the top and bottom edges. This
  image will be stacked directly against the next one, so any empty band at the
  top or bottom breaks the building.
- Far left edge band (about 7% of the width): an exterior steel stair and fire
  escape tower, running vertically off the top and bottom of the frame. Same
  position and width in every floor.
- Far right edge band (about 7% of the width): an elevator shaft with a lit car,
  alongside vertical pipe runs and conduit, running vertically off the top and
  bottom of the frame. Same position and width in every floor.
- Between them, the interior of this one storey, divided into five or six rooms
  by interior partition walls.
- The stair tower and elevator shaft pass straight through this floor and
  continue to the floors above and below. The exterior shell never changes.
